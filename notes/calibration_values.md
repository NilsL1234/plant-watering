# Calibration of pumping duration:

## How much water does the pump deliver?

- 360 sec: around 85 ml

- 600 sec: around 140 ml

- so the pump delivers around 14 ml per minute


## The plant needs around 250 ml when the soil is completly dry. The watering cycle has 1 or 2 pumps. How long is one pump?

- cycle: 250 ml
- pump: 125 ml

- 125ml / 14ml/min = 8,9 min per pump

- One pumping is 9 min long.
